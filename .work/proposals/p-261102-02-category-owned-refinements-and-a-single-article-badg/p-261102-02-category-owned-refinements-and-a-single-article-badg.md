## Proposal

<!-- proposal-body:start -->
## Requested refinement

Tags belong beneath a category and narrow that category's articles. Assign explicit parent categories in the registry and generate publication-scoped static refinement pages. Keep existing article and tag URLs working.

## Presentation trial

Cards, the feature, article headers and search show one colored category disclosure beside the date. Hover or native click/tap/keyboard disclosure reveals the article's specific tags and derived Demo status. Tags use the category hue mixed toward theme ink (lighter in dark mode, readable in light mode). Demo remains canonical boolean editorial metadata; its category filter is derived, not a free-floating subject tag.

Explore expands each category to its own specific tags and Demo filter; remove the separate global tag section. Remove outline/box-shadow rings from icon controls even for native modal autofocus. Preserve keyboard recognition through an internal short underline/stroke cue and text underlines, plus native modality and focus restoration.
