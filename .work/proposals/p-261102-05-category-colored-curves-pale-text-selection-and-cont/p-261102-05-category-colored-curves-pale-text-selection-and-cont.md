## Proposal

## Owner correction
Use the DSP category hue for both time-domain and spectrum traces. Use the optics category hue for both signed Gaussian boundaries, retaining dashed styling for the negative boundary. Apply the correction consistently to previews, static alternatives and live plots.

## Scientific distinction
Category identity does not prohibit multiple scientific colors. Different quantities/comparisons may use distinguishable secondary colors when useful; these two demonstrations use one category hue with their labels, separate panels or line style supplying the distinction.

## Selection and focus
Article text selection uses a pale category/white blend with dark readable selected text in both themes. Global selection remains neutral. Investigate the screenshot frame on the focusable live demo region and other structural/anchor focus targets. Remove browser-default frames around wrappers and programmatic heading/main targets; retain keyboard text underlines, small icon cues and local input/scroll-region cues. Do not disable focus or keyboard access.

## Verification
Inspect desktop/mobile in both themes, programmatic and keyboard focus, live/static/preview trace colors, real text selection and contrast, no-script and engine-failure alternatives. Preserve numerical models, plot proportions, public demos and unrelated working-tree edits.
