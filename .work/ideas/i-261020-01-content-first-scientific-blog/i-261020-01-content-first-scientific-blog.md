## What the thing is

A professional scientific and technical publication with content as the protagonist, an honest interdisciplinary author profile, evidence-rich figures, and a consistent reading/discovery experience.

## What is known

The owner agreed the direction on 2026-10-20. The repository uses Astro, MDX, React islands, and build-time KaTeX. Navigation/category inconsistencies and missing assets were confirmed in source. The prior production build passed; visual browser testing did not complete because system dependencies were unavailable.

## Why it might work

Strong reasoning, reproducible examples, and scientific figures demonstrate ability while keeping articles immediately accessible. About can explain the author without turning the site into a CV landing page.

## What would kill it

A design that obscures writing, invents specialist experience, overloads mobile screens, or turns planning into an indefinitely growing prerequisite for publication.

## Cheapest next step

The requested documentation and ledger foundation is now recorded. Next implement one topic registry and consistent mobile/desktop navigation; inspect a real article and category view before broadening the redesign.
