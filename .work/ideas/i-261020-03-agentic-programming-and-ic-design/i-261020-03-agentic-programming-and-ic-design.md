## What the thing is

Explore agents as programming assistants and active participants in engineering workflows, including integrated circuit design with an open-source ecosystem.

## What is known

This is an owner-stated interest, not evidence of existing IC expertise. No toolchain, PDK, benchmark, or implementation scope has been chosen.

## Why it might work

Bounded tasks with explicit constraints and independent verification can reveal where agent assistance is useful and where human review remains necessary.

## What would kill it

No reproducible baseline, unsuitable tool/PDK licensing, insufficient compute, or results that cannot be independently checked.

## Cheapest next step

Choose one small candidate task and research current primary tool documentation; establish a manually validated baseline before adding an agent.
